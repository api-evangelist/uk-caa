# UK Civil Aviation Authority (CAA)

> The UK’s independent aviation regulator, providing authoritative regulation, licensing, safety guidance, datasets, and passenger information.

The UK Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) regulates civil aviation in the United Kingdom. This site provides official regulatory guidance, licensing services, aviation datasets, consumer protection information, and policy content for passengers, pilots, commercial operators, drone users, and aerospace stakeholders.
